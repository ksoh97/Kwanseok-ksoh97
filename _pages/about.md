I completed my Ph.D. in the [Department of Artificial Intelligence](http://xai.korea.ac.kr/) at [Korea University](https://www.korea.ac.kr/sites/ko/index.do), where I was advised by [Prof. Heung-Il Suk](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dl_oZLwAAAAJ&hl=ko) in the [Machine Intelligence Lab (MILAB)](https://milab.korea.ac.kr/). My research interests span generalizable and foundational modeling for medical and computer vision, vision-language models, explainable AI, representation learning, and biomedical image analysis.

To learn more, take a look at my [publications](/Kwanseok-ksoh97/publications/) and [research interests](/Kwanseok-ksoh97/).
